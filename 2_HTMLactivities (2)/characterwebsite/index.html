My Name Is Sasuke Uchiha

The shadow kage of the hidden leaf village

Who Is He?

Sasuke Uchiha is one of the main characters in the anime Naruto. The show was created by Masahi Kishimoto. In the first series he is depicted to be a child seeking revenge for his clan who he lost as a young boy. he teams up with the other main characters of the show, naruto and sakura, to train and learn how to control their abilities as shinobi's. he is known for his serious personality, his unwavering want for revenge and his speacial ability which he inherited from the Uchiha clan...

What His Story Is

Going more into depth of his backstory, Sasuke became an ophan at the age of 6 years old. his parents along with the entirety of his clan, was murdered by his older brother Itatchi. Filled with rage Sasuke decides to take it upon himself to not only kill his brother but the entire group he is associated with,the "Akatsuki Clan". They are known as a terriorist group in the anime, 10 members, all with special abilities worked together to collect the "tailed beasts". One of which was sealed inside Naruto. As the series goes on it shows Sasuke battling himself as he workds through grief, friendship, and discovering if he really knew the truth about his own family.

What Are His Abilities?
  • Sharingan: able to see and track the opponents moves easier, read an opponents movements, copy any technique after seeing it, and cast as well as resist genjustsu (illusion techniques)
  • Amaterasu: creates continuous black flames that will burn the opponent and destroy the target until he extnguishes it.
  • Kagutsuchi: Uses the amaterasu but he is able to mold and shape the flames to create a barrier or weapons
  • Susanoo: creates a giant chakra warrior that evelopes and shields him, uses it as offense and defense
  • Chidori: concentrates a ball of lightning into his hand to use as a strike method in battle

These are only a few of Sasuke's many abilitues but throughouts the show you can see how much he had grown with learning to not only perfecting his old skills but also learning the dangers of how powerful he really is.

His Character Development

As said earlier Sasuke is driven with revenge and his need to become powerful. he joins the villian in the story, "orochimaru" believing that he will be helpful in terms of finding his brother and killing him, so he abandons his close friends, his home, and his mentor, believing that he cannot rest until his brother is dead. that all changed when he finally accomplishes his life long goal, but he learns the truth behind why Itachi did what he did, and that sends him into another spiral. The show ranges from when he was 12-13 all the way until he was in his 20's, and throughout it all, we see him change from being power hungy, vengeful, and angry, to finally making peace with himself and coming to terms with his inner turmoil. in the final series of the anime, he even gets his version of a happy ending, where he returned to the village and has a wife and daughter!
