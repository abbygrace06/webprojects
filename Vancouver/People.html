The nice thing about living in BC, more specifically Vancouver, is the community of residents it has. There are approximately 700,000 people within the city itself, while the larger metro Vancouver region has around 3 million people. Of course bigger cities on a large scale such as Vancouver is not everyone's preference, but i think it isn't the large buildings or sparkling water, but also the people that make up the city. There are all sorts of communities and cliques that could potentially fit your aesthetic or lifestyle. The residents for the most part are very friendly. Many assume because of how large the city is, it is hard to make a real connection with the people around you. However, it may seem like the city and the people are constantly moving, if you took the time to have a chat or even say hi, you might end up hearing some fascinating stories. After living in a smaller city in southern Alberta and comparing both experiences, I found I am still drawn to BC and a big reason for that is the people that live there. You can find “hostility”, or the one having a bad day, anywhere you go, but I found that I experienced more of it in a smaller city. Vancouver may be crowded, but there is never a shortage of the diversity that lives within it. I remember when I visited last year, I spent about 2 hours talking to my tattoo artist. He told me about his life, how he ended up where he was, and his aspirations for his future. I thought that was one of the best talks I've had with a person, and the funny thing about it was that I was actually nervous to start the initial conversation. All it takes is a quick hello to get to know someone. A lot of the time, if you do that with Vancouver's residents, they will be more than happy to talk back. If you are someone that actually prefers to keep to yourself and you have had a long day and want to just sit in silence on the bus, the people of Vancouver give that to you as well. The fast pace of the city can be a positive, because everyone can be wrapped up in their own world, that means you can be too, without the interruption of others.
