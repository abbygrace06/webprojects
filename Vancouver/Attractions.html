Time to get into the fun part of Vancouver! Compared to a smaller city, one of the advantages of living here would be the wide variety of attractions. There are many restaurants located downtown, one of my favourites is Rio Brazilian Steakhouse. Not only is it a family friendly atmosphere but it is a super fun experience with a beautiful view of english bay! Speaking of beaches, it goes without saying that Vancouver has some of the most fantastic views on the west coast. You are guaranteed a breathtaking view if you visit the local beaches such as English bay, Kitsilano beach, sunset beach, and many more! Each beach has its perks. English bay and second beach connect making it stretch about 3.2 miles. Of course you have the warm sand and crystal water to enjoy but you can also enjoy the scenic walk along the beach. On one side you get the stunning expanse of the ocean, and just behind you, is the sparkling lights of the city. Say you are not sold on staying in the city 24/7, well there is things to do for you too! Vancouver is built within some of Canada's most stunning mountains. You can indulge yourself on a getaway trip through the rockies, or spend a night camping by the lake.
