Vancouver is a beautiful city in British Columbia. It is located along the west coast, that means if you love being by the water, Vancouver should be the right place for you! The city is always bustling with activity, sparkling lights, and the opportunity to have interactions you might never have thought of. Visiting this city will allow you to make connections and create friendships that will stick with you for a lifetime, along with making countless memories, of course. That is only some of the perks of living or visiting this city, so why else should you?
