In terms of work, Vancouver offers all types of jobs for you to gain experience, start a future in, or just a general position to earn money. For young adults or teenagers the most common occupation to get into is either a retail or a fast food job. Many people I know, as well as myself, worked in places like cineplex, the mall, or a hostess position at restaurants like cactus club, earls, and boston pizza. Jobs like that were very common to have while we were in high school or even after. The hours were flexible and although it was minimum wage, what 15 year old is going to say no to any sort of paycheque when it is pizza day at school? Some sort of office position is also common. Many people have transitioned into entrepreneurship, that way you can work on your own time, you are able to do it wherever you are, and you can be your own boss! Some of the major companies that offer positions like a receptionist, customer-service representative, or an HR assistant, are Vancouver Coastal Health, Deloitte and KPMG, and RBC. If sitting at a desk is not for you, there are endless possibilities from being a babysitter, to assisting in film making. The nice thing about how many opportunities there are is that it gives you a chance to jump around and try out different jobs, until something fits. While you do that you could make connections with others that might open a new door.
