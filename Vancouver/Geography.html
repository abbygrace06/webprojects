Since British Columbia is located along the west coast, the stunning views are not only because of the tall skyscrapers, but of the mountains that surround it. No matter where you are or what viewpoint you are looking from, the mountains will always be a sight to see. Of course forests come with that. Vancouver is known for its lush forests, where families and friends can go to spend time together, surrounded by the fresh air, away from the city craziness and find a spot by the water. One thing I always appreciated about Vancouver is, even though it can seem to be packed with people, housing, or new developments, the city does its best to preserve the wildlife and its forests. It tries to incorporate the terrain and build around it rather than on top of it...most of the time. You also have the bodies of water surrounding the city. I find the water is the most beautiful in the summer time, especially when the glow of sunset hits the surface. Vancouver is known as the “rain-couver” and the name speaks for itself. Even though the city experiences all the changes of the season, it does rain roughly 160-170 days each year. That does vary between what feels like a heavy rain storm or a spring shower. Compared to southern provinces, Vancouver can seem “colder” because of how moist the air is.
